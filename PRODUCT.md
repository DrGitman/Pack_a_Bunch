# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People packing things into a fixed space: moving house, loading a car boot for a trip, fitting
stock onto a shelf, filling a crate or a storage box. They are standing next to the pile with
their phone, usually in a hurry, and have already packed and unpacked at least once.

## Product Purpose

Pack a Bunch (Android) measures the space and the things with the phone's camera — no tape
measure — plans where every piece goes in 3D, then guides the packing step by step
("lay it flat, push it into the back right corner"). If something doesn't fit, it asks why and
takes you straight to the fix. Free plans one pack of up to 20 pieces; Pack Plus (weekly or
monthly) unlocks unlimited packs, pieces, scans and any size of space.

Success: someone packs a car boot once, first time, and it all fits.

## The one workflow to show

Scan the space (car boot) → scan the things on the table (several at once, each outlined and
measured) → the 3D plan → the step-by-step guide → it fits.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, quietly clever. Three words: warm, exact, reassuring.
Colours: warm ground #F7EFE6, deep brown #3F2718, terracotta #A65C34, camera amber #F2A03D.

## Anti-references

Not a generic blue SaaS explainer. No stock photos of smiling movers. No feature laundry list.
No login or loading screens.

## Design Principles

1. Open on the pain (a pile of things, one boot, "will it fit?") within 3 seconds.
2. The "aha" — point the phone, it's measured — before 30 seconds.
3. One complete workflow, end to end, as benefits not features.
4. Captions on everything; it must work with the sound off.
5. End on one low-friction call to action: "Try Pack Plus — code on the Pack Plus page".
