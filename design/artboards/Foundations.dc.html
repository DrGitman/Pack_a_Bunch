Shape & space
Cards 22–28 px, sheets 30 px, everything pressable fully rounded. Screen gutters 20 px, 4/8/12/16/24 spacing. Shadows are warm and low: 0 8px 22px rgba(90,58,35,.08).
Android, not iOS
Frames are 412 × 916. The top 30 px and the bottom gesture strip are left empty for the real system bars — no painted status bar, no notch, no iOS back chevron in a title.
Say what is true
“Modelled fill”, not “optimised”. “Camera estimate”, never a confidence score. “No arrangement found”, never “impossible”. No percentage without a stated basis.
Reachable
Nothing under 44 px tall. Items carry a number, a name and a colour so colour is never the only signal. Every camera path has a typed path beside it.
