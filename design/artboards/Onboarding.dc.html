Pack a Bunch
What this does,
and what it doesn't
Thirty seconds now saves a wrong assumption later.
Boxy spaces, open at the top
Crates, storage boxes, drawers, a car boot. You measure the space inside and keep the opening clear.
Firm things, up to 20 pieces
Every item gets a width, depth and height — measured with the camera or typed in. Then you get an order to pack them in.
Not yet: soft, odd or heavy
Backpacks, duvets, awkward shapes, and whether a stack will take the weight. Where we can't tell, we say so instead of guessing.
Better to know now than half way through a move.
